## 2026-06-24 — update (primitive pivot: least-squares circle → facet uniformity)

The curvature-segmentation / least-squares-circle primitive was implemented and **empirically hit its make-or-break** on the real fixture (bench-deployed, inspected in the visualizer): arc count stuck at ~4 / max 2 mm at any `deviation_tol` 0.005–4 mm, the center circle never fit, and loose tol both missed real arcs and admitted straights. Per-facet measurement showed why: "arc" bands fuse straight wall edges (a 9.86 mm straight inside one half-circle band) and corners, and genuine arc chords swing ±~23% — so least-squares circle-fitting is structurally the wrong gate.

Pivot (user's insight): detect arcs by **facet uniformity** — constant chord length, constant turn angle, constant extrusion-per-mm, each ±tol (constant length+angle is the circle's chord form; constant E/mm confines to one printed feature). Reworked SPEC accordingly: Why rewritten; CAP-1..3 preserved (intents unchanged, success wording aligned); **CAP-4 revised** from `{deviation_tol, min_run}` to the uniformity tolerances `(length_tol, angle_tol, epmm_tol, min_run)`; **CAP-5 added** (E/mm confinement). Constraints replaced the deviation-tolerance class-separator with the uniformity rule + running-average comparison (to tolerate the ±23% swing). New load-bearing constraint: the offline harness zeroes extrusion and must be fixed to carry it before CAP-5 is testable. Title → "Arc detection by facet uniformity"; least-squares fitting moved to an explicit non-goal.
