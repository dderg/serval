## 2026-06-17 — design discussion, consolidated (the big one)

Worked through the "settled" claims one by one with dderg, pressure-testing each. The verbatim brain-dump companion was deleted earlier at dderg's request ("discuss, then formalize"); this entry records what we actually decided, now written into the rewritten `SPEC.md` and the new authored `architecture.md` companion. Two Explore agents read the current Rust tree (`rust/`) to ground the discussion in reality.

**What the code investigation changed.** The extruder is already a follower (`FollowerDemand`, `try_new_virtual`, odometer over the post-shaping signal) — my "extruder breaks κ(s)" objection was moot. The current TOPP is *not* "TOPP on raw Bézier-t" as the map claimed — it reparameterizes to arc length (1024-pt table) and solves a curvature-based convex SOCP, then SLP9 jerk refinement, then iterative joining. So this is a restructuring, and the dominant non-real-time cost is **jerk-in-the-convex-core (SLP9)**, not the representation.

**Decisions reached:**
- Representation: typed `Segment { Line | Arc | Clothoid }` + follower channel. κ is a *derived* field, not the generative truth (typed segments are). Justification for curvature = "it's the coordinate the constraint is written in," not the (planar, oversold) fundamental theorem. Clothoid: κ(s) closed-form (design space), Fresnel only at execution lowering.
- Blend shape decided: **clothoid**. The accel vector rides the limit at magnitude a_max and rotates (braking→centripetal→forward) — vector-budget trading is why it beats an arc on time; lateral jerk is bounded by the geometry for free.
- Fitter is **shaper-agnostic by authority** (not a bet/approximation). Planner never caps accel for ringing; max_accel is the user's sovereign knob. This *reverses* the map's "shaper-aware middleware" and drops the post-shaper-within-δ goal (now a decided, recoverable non-goal). Fitter inputs: δ bounds geometry, max_accel decides blend-or-not + speed.
- Jerk split: lateral = free (clothoid geometry); tangential = **1-D S-curve in the velocity lookahead, not a post-processor** (the no-room accel→decel reversal kills the post-processor). Jerk lives in s(t), never geometry → no artifacts; fail loudly if a segment can't absorb its smoothing.
- Velocity planning: decoupling lateral (geometry caps) from tangential (1-D) **removes the SOCP** in favor of forward-backward integration + S-curve ramps over closed-form clothoid caps. The velocity profile over a clothoid is an *output* of the global sweep (all-decel / all-accel / dip / cruise). Tighter-next-feature = ordinary backward propagation; coupling horizon = window, reset at full stops.
- Acceleration continuity = fitter guarantees κ-continuity (lateral) + shared boundary node (tangential); fitter guarantees collinearity except move starts.
- SCV reused as the knob, angle dependence from closed-form `κ_peak(angle, δ)`.
- Shaper/PA stay execution post-processors; follower advances on post-shaper velocity (already coded). PA-profile simplification = TODO after basic movement.
- Build: constant-speed-per-feature skeleton first, then limit-riding through clothoids as the measurable upgrade.

**Open questions now tracked in SPEC.md:** jerk-limit floor; `κ_peak(angle, δ)`; **forward-backward sweep convergence/real-time (the last unverified load-bearing claim)**; ride-limit-vs-cap switch-on; execution lowering (Fresnel degree/tolerance); dumb-gcode chain detection; back-to-back-clothoid dκ/ds step; helical G2/G3 support; extruder post-IS simplification.

## 2026-06-17 — jerk ownership resolved; fitter = continuous-κ(s) optimizer

Worked the three scenarios dderg posed (straight→clothoid; clothoid entered below cap; chain of different-size clothoids). Key fact: lateral accel `a_c = v²κ`.

**Decided — solver owns jerk; fitter is a cost-reducing optimizer; they compose (one does not cancel the other).**
- Tangential jerk → S-curve (already). Any *residual* κ-step the fitter can't remove → closed-form velocity cap `v ≤ √(a/κ)` at the step. Because `a_c=v²κ`, slowing shrinks the accel-step → graceful degradation to mainline corner-capping, never fail-loud. Stays decoupled (precomputed caps, no SOCP).
- Refinement of dderg's scenario 3: differently-sized clothoids with *matched* κ give a finite lateral-*jerk* step (accepted as G2, like line→clothoid), **not** an acceleration discontinuity. The accel discontinuity appears only when κ itself can't be matched (no room) — and that's exactly what the speed cap handles.

**Fitter design direction (dderg's "better shape through the entire thing"):** a clothoid is "linear κ(s)", so a chain of corners is one **continuous, piecewise-linear κ(s) profile** (clothoid spline) within δ of the whole polyline. Through a dense chain κ stays continuous and need not drop to zero between neighbors → removes accel discontinuity, avoids inter-corner slowdown (faster), deletes the overlap special-case. Out of room ⇒ κ profile stays elevated (smaller/merged), not a collision. Same problem as faceted-chain-vs-corner detection.

**Build ladder:** v1 = per-corner blends + solver safety net (step 4–6). Later = global continuous-κ chain fit (new step 8), measurable upgrade, doesn't touch the solver.

**Resolved Open Questions:** fitter-vs-solver jerk ownership (RESOLVED); back-to-back-clothoid dκ/ds step (subsumed by continuous-κ fit); geometric overlap (graceful, not fail-loud). New Open Question: the global continuous-κ(s) chain-fit algorithm, coupled to dumb-gcode chain detection.

## 2026-06-18 — chain-fit formulation + line↔arc transition spiral

Tackled the global continuous-κ(s) chain-fit / faceted-vs-corner open question.

**Key collapse — there is no classifier.** You only have the polyline, not slicer intent, and don't need it. Fit *the gentlest κ within the δ-tube around the polyline* and the facet/corner distinction falls out: a faceted arc → tube hugs the arc → fit ≈ constant κ; a sharp corner → δ binds → κ peaks at κ_peak(δ). Same objective, no `if`, no threshold to tune.

**Formulation (tangent-angle space).** φ(s)=tangent angle, κ=dφ/ds; polyline = staircase φ_poly jumping θ_i per vertex. Deviation ≈ ∫(φ−φ_poly)ds (convex tube). Restrict to clothoid-spline family, minimise ∫(dκ/ds)² — minimising curvature *rate* selects linear-κ (clothoid) pieces → banded, effectively non-iterative QP. ∫(κ′)² is physical, not arbitrary: lateral jerk ∝ v³κ′, so the fit objective IS a lateral-jerk-energy minimiser — the gentlest geometry the machine can run. Output κ(s) is piecewise-linear = exactly the {Line|Arc|Clothoid} alphabet, no new type. Locality: vertices couple only within δ-bounded blend reach → O(n) chain detection (L_c(i)+L_c(i+1) < segment ⇒ anchor), fitter revision horizon δ-bounded, symmetric to the velocity braking horizon.

**Line↔arc seams = a clothoid half (dderg's addition).** Single clothoid ramping κ 0→κ_arc (the classic road/rail transition spiral), not a symmetric pair. Line→arc→line = trapezoidal κ(s). The insert shifts the arc inward (road-design offset p), must fit within δ. Insert-or-not is the same speed-cap economics: gentle arc → small κ_arc → cheap a_c=v²κ_arc step → passes through; tight arc → pays → earns entry/exit halves. Makes CAP-3's "tight arcs get transitions, gentle arcs pass" quantitative; covers explicit G2/G3 slicer arc seams too.

**Still open (written into SPEC):** (a) exact objective+solver vs real-time budget; (b) multi-segment δ→κ mapping (coupled generalisation of κ_peak(θ,δ)); (c) fitter horizon constant; (d) measure-before-build gate (v1 = per-corner; build global fit only if the measured gap justifies the QP — a local, geometry-only, off-hot-path solver reintroduction, measured not assumed).

**Grounding recommendation:** this is the CNC corner-smoothing / transition-spiral literature (G01 toolpaths → C²/C³ within confined tolerance, feedrate optimised separately = our decoupling). Ground the closed forms (κ_peak, arc-shift p, transition geometry) there rather than re-derive. Research pass not yet run.
